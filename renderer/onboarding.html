Welcome to Islet

A Dynamic Island for your PC. It lives at the top of your screen and brings your scores, music, calendar and alerts to one place. Setup takes about a minute, and every step is optional.

Football, F1 and NBALive scores, goal and kick-off alerts, standings, race countdowns
Spotify and musicNow playing, controls, playlists, and your Windows media
Calendar and Claude CodeNext meeting with one-click Join, and Claude’s activity at a glance

What do you want on Islet?

Pick what you’d like. Anything you leave off stays off, and you can change this anytime in Settings.

Music, calendar, clipboard and notifications stay on either way.

Your football teams

Follow up to 6 teams. You’ll get a live score pill while they play, plus kick-off, goal and full-time alerts.

Faster live data with API-Football (optional)
Without a key Islet uses ESPN, which works out of the box. A free API-Football key (100 requests a day, get one here) is used first, and Islet falls back to ESPN if the quota runs low.

Your F1

Pick up to 3 drivers and a team. They’re highlighted in the standings and shown on the Now tab.

Your NBA teams

Follow up to 5 teams. Their games lead the NBA tab. Teams load from the standings, so this works without a key.

Connect Spotify

Control playback, like songs and start playlists from the island. Spotify needs a free developer app for this (one minute, and your data stays on your PC).

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard and create an app. Choose “Web API”.
  2. Add this Redirect URI to the app:
    http://127.0.0.1:47615/spotify/callback
  3. Paste the app’s Client ID here and connect:
Playback controls need Spotify Premium. Showing what’s playing works on any account.
